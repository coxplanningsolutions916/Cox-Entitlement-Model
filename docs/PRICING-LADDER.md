# Lifetime payment schedule for the model

Draft 1 October 2026 for Chris's review. The rule behind it: **the client pays for each depth of the model once, never re-pays for a fact already established, and every later fee carries the model's upkeep inside it.** The model is opened at the first paid step and stays with the project to closeout.

## What "included in the project management fee" means in a scope

Every task order's coordination line reads, in substance: *Project coordination (10% of labor) includes maintaining the project Roadmap model: monthly schedule and budget refresh, assumption register updates as deliverables land, change notices for rule or map changes affecting the site, and the client statement.* That sentence is what makes the model a billed, expected part of every project rather than a free extra.

## Rules

1. One price per depth. A client who buys the Roadmap after a free launch screen pays $2,500, not $2,000; the credit is for a paid screen only.
2. A second site for the same client is a new 1A at $500 unless it is part of the same assemblage.
3. Re-screening a stale 1A (more than 12 months old, or the jurisdiction changed its rules) is a new 1A.
4. No fee ever re-establishes a fact the model already holds. If a later task order would re-do work, the register says so and the scope excludes it.
5. The Roadmap always ends with a priced Step 1b proposal; the proposal is part of the $2,500, not a separate sales step.
